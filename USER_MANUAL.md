## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Name of the System
**Dreamy School Management System**

### 1.2 Purpose
The Dreamy School Management System is designed to help schools manage:
- Student enrollment and admission
- Academic records and schedules
- Financial transactions and payment tracking
- Document management
- Communication between staff, teachers, and students

### 1.3 Target Users

| User Type | Description | Access Method |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Applicants** | Prospective students applying for enrollment | Web Browser |
| **Students** | Enrolled students | Mobile App |
| **Teachers** | Faculty members | Mobile App |
| **Registrar** | Enrollment and student management staff | Desktop/Web Portal |
| **Administrator** | System administrators | Desktop/Web Portal |

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 How to Access the System

#### For Applicants and Web Portal Users
1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge recommended)
2. Navigate to your school's Dreamy portal URL (e.g., `https://your-school.dreamy.edu`)
3. You will see the school homepage with admission information

#### For Students (Mobile App)
1. Download the **Dreamy Student App** from:
   - Google Play Store (Android)
   - Apple App Store (iOS)
2. Or use the Expo Go app and scan the QR code provided by your school
3. Open the app and proceed to login

#### For Teachers (Mobile App)
1. Download the **Dreamy App** (same app as students)
2. Teachers are automatically directed to the teacher portal after login

### 2.2 Account Creation and Login

#### For New Applicants
1. Go to the school website
2. Click **"Apply Now"** or **"Register"**
3. Fill in your email address
4. Create a password (minimum 8 characters)
5. Click **"Create Account"**
6. Check your email for a verification link
7. Click the verification link to activate your account
8. Return to the login page and sign in

#### For Students and Teachers
Your account is created by the school administration. You will receive:
- An email with your login credentials
- Or an invitation link to set up your password

#### Logging In
1. Enter your registered email address
2. Enter your password
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. If PIN is enabled, enter your 6-digit PIN

### 2.3 Setting Up PIN Security (Mobile App)

After your first login, you may be prompted to set up a PIN:

1. **Create a PIN**: Enter a 6-digit number
2. **Confirm PIN**: Re-enter the same 6-digit number
3. **Remember your PIN**: This will be required for future logins and sensitive operations

> **Tip:** Choose a PIN you can remember but others cannot easily guess. Avoid using birthdays or simple sequences like 123456.

## 5. Applicant Guide

### 5.1 Creating an Account

1. Visit the school website
2. Click **"Apply Now"** or **"Register"**
3. Complete the registration form:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email Address (use a valid email you can access)
   - Password
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Check your email inbox for verification
6. Click the verification link

### 5.2 Completing the Application Form

After logging in as an applicant:

1. Click **"Start Application"** or **"Application Form"**
2. Complete all sections:

**Section 1: Personal Information**
- Full legal name
- Birthdate and place of birth
- Gender
- Contact number
- Address (current and permanent)

**Section 2: Academic Preferences**
- Grade level applying for
- Preferred track (Academic, TVL, etc.)
- Preferred strand/program
- Preferred schedule (if applicable)

**Section 3: Parent/Guardian Information**
- Father's information
- Mother's information
- Guardian's information (if applicable)

**Section 4: Educational Background**
- Last school attended
- Last grade level completed
- School year completed

**Section 5: Additional Information**
- Special needs (if any)
- 4Ps beneficiary status
- Indigenous People status

3. Review all information
4. Click **"Submit Application"**

### 5.3 Uploading Required Documents

After submitting your application:

1. Go to **"Document Submission"** or check your dashboard
2. You will see a list of required documents:
   - Birth Certificate
   - Report Card / Form 138
   - Good Moral Character
   - Medical Certificate
   - ID Photos
   - Other documents as required

3. For each document:
   - Click **"Upload"** or the upload button
   - Select the file from your device
   - Wait for upload to complete
   - Status will change to "Submitted"

**Document Tips:**
- Use clear, readable scans or photos
- Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG
- Maximum file size: Usually 5 MB per file
- Ensure all text is legible

### 5.4 Checking Application Status

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Dashboard** or **Application Status**
3. View your current status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Application is being reviewed |
| **Documents Pending** | Additional documents needed |
| **Accepted** | Application approved, awaiting enrollment |
| **Rejected** | Application was not approved |

### 5.5 Interview Scheduling (if applicable)

If your school requires an interview:

1. You will receive a notification when scheduled
2. Check your dashboard for interview details:
   - Date and time
   - Location
   - Instructions
3. Attend the interview as scheduled

## 6. Student Mobile App Guide

### 6.1 Logging In

1. Open the Dreamy App
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Tap **"Sign In"**
5. If prompted, enter or set up your PIN

### 6.2 Dashboard Screen

The Dashboard is your home screen showing:

**Student Information Card:**
- Your name and profile picture
- LRN (Learner Reference Number)
- Grade level
- Program/Strand
- Enrollment status

**Enrollment Status & Actions:**
- Current enrollment status
- Confirm enrollment button (when available)
- Re-enrollment information

**Quick Stats:**
- Recent activities
- Important dates

**News & Announcements:**
- Latest school news
- Important announcements

### 6.3 Confirming Enrollment

When your enrollment is ready for confirmation:

1. Go to **Dashboard**
2. You will see a **"Confirm Enrollment"** button
3. Tap the button
4. Confirm your action in the popup
5. Your status will update to "Confirmed"

> **Important:** Confirm your enrollment within the specified period to secure your slot.

### 6.4 Academic Information

**Accessing Academic Info:**
1. Tap the **Academic** tab (book icon)
2. Choose between two views:
   - **Subjects** - Your enrolled subjects
   - **Sections** - Your class section details

**Subjects View:**
Shows all your enrolled subjects:
- Subject name
- Teacher name
- Schedule (days and time)
- Room number

**Section View:**
Shows your section information:
- Section name
- Program
- Year level
- Adviser

### 6.5 Financial Information

**Accessing Financial Info:**
1. Tap the **Financial** tab (credit card icon)
2. Choose between two views:
   - **Invoices** - Your fee breakdowns
   - **Payments** - Your payment history

**Understanding Your Invoice:**
- **Invoice Number** - Unique identifier
- **Total Amount** - Full amount due
- **Paid Amount** - Amount already paid
- **Balance** - Remaining amount to pay
- **Status** - Unpaid, Partially Paid, Paid

**Selecting a Payment Plan:**
1. Open your invoice
2. Review available payment options:
   - **Full Payment** - Pay entire amount
   - **Installment Plan** - Pay in monthly installments
3. Select your preferred plan
4. Review the payment schedule
5. Confirm your selection

**Viewing Payment History:**
1. Go to Financial > Payments
2. View all recorded payments:
   - Payment date
   - Amount paid
   - Payment method
   - Reference number

### 6.6 Notifications

**Accessing Notifications:**
1. Tap the **Updates** tab (bell icon)
2. View all your notifications

**Notification Types:**
- 📢 School announcements
- 💰 Payment reminders
- 📝 Enrollment updates
- 📄 Document status updates

**Managing Notifications:**
- Tap a notification to view details
- Unread notifications show a blue dot
- Tap **"Mark All Read"** to clear all unread

### 6.7 Profile Management

**Viewing Your Profile:**
1. Tap the **Profile** tab (person icon)
2. View your information:
   - Name and photo
   - Student status
   - Contact information

**Editing Personal Information:**
1. Go to Profile
2. Tap **"Personal Information"**
3. Tap **"Edit"** to make changes
4. Update your information:
   - Contact number
   - Address
   - Emergency contacts
5. Tap **"Save"** to apply changes

**Account Settings:**
1. Go to Profile
2. Tap **"Account Settings"**
3. Available options:
   - Change Password
   - Change Email
   - PIN Management
   - Enable/Disable PIN

### 6.8 Changing Your Password

1. Go to Profile > Account Settings
2. Under **Change Password**:
   - Enter current password
   - Enter new password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm new password
   - Enter PIN (if enabled)
3. Tap **"Change Password"**
4. Success message will appear

### 6.9 Managing Your PIN

**Changing PIN:**
1. Go to Profile > Account Settings
2. Under **PIN Management**:
   - Enter current PIN
   - Enter new 6-digit PIN
   - Confirm new PIN
3. Tap **"Change PIN"**

**Enabling/Disabling PIN:**
1. Go to Profile > Account Settings
2. Under **PIN Security**:
   - Toggle PIN on/off
   - Enter current PIN to confirm
3. Changes apply immediately

### 6.10 Logging Out

1. Go to Profile
2. Scroll to bottom
3. Tap **"Logout"**
4. Confirm logout in popup
5. You will return to login screen

## 8. Admin/Registrar Web Portal Guide

### 8.1 Accessing the Admin Portal

1. Open web browser
2. Go to your school's Dreamy URL
3. Click **"Portal Login"** or **"Admin Login"**
4. Enter credentials
5. If PIN enabled, enter 6-digit PIN
6. You will be directed to the Admin Dashboard

### 8.2 Dashboard Overview

The Admin Dashboard displays:

**Statistics Cards:**
- Total applicants
- Pending applications
- Enrolled students
- Revenue summary

**Quick Actions:**
- Add new academic term
- View pending applications
- Recent activities

**Charts:**
- Enrollment trends
- Application statistics
- Financial overview

### 8.3 Managing Applications

#### Viewing Pending Applications
1. Go to **Applications** > **Pending**
2. View list of pending applications
3. Click on any row to view details

#### Reviewing an Application
1. Open a pending application
2. Review all submitted information:
   - Personal details
   - Academic preferences
   - Family information
   - Submitted documents

3. Check document status:
   - ✅ Verified - Document is accepted
   - ⏳ Pending - Awaiting review
   - ❌ Rejected - Needs resubmission

#### Accepting an Application
1. Open the application
2. Click **"Accept & Schedule"**
3. Set interview details (if required):
   - Date
   - Time
   - Location
4. Click **"Confirm"**
5. Applicant receives notification

#### Recording Admission Result
1. Go to **Applications** > **Accepted**
2. Find the applicant
3. Click **"Record Result"**
4. Select result:
   - **Passed** - Ready for enrollment
   - **Failed** - Application rejected
5. Add remarks if needed
6. Click **"Save"**

#### Rejecting an Application
1. Open the application
2. Click **"Reject"**
3. Select reason:
   - Incomplete documents
   - Does not meet requirements
   - Other (specify)
4. Add remarks
5. Confirm rejection
6. Applicant receives notification

### 8.4 Managing Documents

#### Viewing Submitted Documents
1. Go to **Applications** > **Pending Documents**
2. Or open any application and go to Documents tab
3. View all submitted files

#### Verifying Documents
1. Click on a document to view/download
2. Review the document
3. Click **"Approve"** or **"Reject"**
4. If rejecting, provide reason
5. Status updates automatically

#### Managing Required Documents
1. Go to **Documents** menu
2. View list of document types
3. Add new document type:
   - Click **"Add Document"**
   - Enter document name
   - Set requirements
   - Save

## 10. Error Messages and Solutions

### 10.1 Login Errors

| Error Message | Cause | Solution |
|---------------|-------|----------|
| "Invalid credentials" | Wrong email or password | Double-check your email and password. Use "Forgot Password" if needed. |
| "Account not verified" | Email not verified | Check your email for verification link. Click "Resend Verification" if needed. |
| "Account is inactive" | Account disabled | Contact school administration. |
| "Too many login attempts" | Multiple failed attempts | Wait 1-5 minutes before trying again. |
| "Invalid PIN" | Wrong PIN entered | Enter correct PIN. After 5 attempts, wait 1 minute. |

### 10.2 Mobile App Errors

| Error Message | Cause | Solution |
|---------------|-------|----------|
| "Network request failed" | No internet connection | Check your WiFi or mobile data connection. |
| "Unable to load dashboard" | Server connection issue | Pull down to refresh. Check internet connection. |
| "Session expired" | Token expired | Log in again. |
| "Server error" | Backend issue | Wait a few minutes and try again. Contact support if persists. |

### 10.3 Document Upload Errors

| Error Message | Cause | Solution |
|---------------|-------|----------|
| "File too large" | Exceeds size limit | Compress the file or reduce image quality. |
| "Invalid file type" | Wrong format | Use PDF, JPG, or PNG format. |
| "Upload failed" | Connection issue | Check internet and try again. |

### 10.4 Payment Errors

| Error Message | Cause | Solution |
|---------------|-------|----------|
| "Amount exceeds balance" | Paying more than owed | Enter correct amount (up to balance due). |
| "Invalid PIN" | Wrong PIN | Enter correct PIN. |
| "Payment plan already exists" | Duplicate plan | Remove existing plan first if changing. |

### 10.5 What to Do If...

**The app is not loading:**
1. Close the app completely
2. Check your internet connection
3. Reopen the app
4. Try logging in again
5. If issue persists, uninstall and reinstall

**You forgot your PIN:**
1. Contact the school registrar
2. Request PIN reset
3. Set up new PIN on next login

**Documents won't upload:**
1. Check file size (under 5 MB)
2. Use correct format (PDF, JPG, PNG)
3. Try a different file
4. Use a stable WiFi connection

**You can't find your invoice:**
1. Check the Financial tab
2. Make sure you're in the correct academic term
3. Contact the finance office

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Questions

**Q: What is the Dreamy School Management System?**
A: It's a digital platform that helps manage student enrollment, academics, finances, and communications between the school and students.

**Q: Do I need to pay to use this system?**
A: The system itself is provided by your school. There's no separate fee for using it.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
A: Yes! Students and teachers can use the mobile app. Administrators use the web portal.

### Account Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions to reset it via email.

**Q: I forgot my PIN. What do I do?**
A: Contact your school's registrar office to request a PIN reset.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes, go to Profile > Account Settings and follow the steps to change your email.

**Q: Why do I need a PIN?**
A: The PIN adds extra security to protect your account, especially for sensitive operations like viewing financial information.

### Application Questions

**Q: How long does the application review take?**
A: Usually 3-7 business days, depending on your school's process.

**Q: What if my application is rejected?**
A: You will receive a notification with the reason. You may reapply in the next enrollment period or contact the school for clarification.

**Q: Can I edit my application after submitting?**
A: Contact the registrar's office to request changes.

**Q: What documents do I need to submit?**
A: Typically: Birth Certificate, Report Card, Good Moral Certificate, Medical Certificate, and ID photos. Check your application for the complete list.

### Student Questions

**Q: How do I confirm my enrollment?**
A: When your enrollment is ready, a "Confirm Enrollment" button will appear on your dashboard. Tap it to confirm.

**Q: How do I see my class schedule?**
A: Go to Academic tab > Subjects. Each subject shows the days, time, and room.

**Q: How do I pay my tuition?**
A: Choose your payment plan in the Financial section. Then, make payments at the school's cashier or designated payment center.

**Q: Why is my balance different from my classmates?**
A: Balances may vary due to discounts, payment plan selections, or program-specific fees.

### Teacher Questions

**Q: How do I see my teaching schedule?**
A: Open the app and your schedule appears on the Dashboard/Schedule tab.

**Q: How do I evaluate students?**
A: Open a class, tap a student, then tap "Evaluate" to mark them as passed or failed.

**Q: Can I evaluate students from previous terms?**
A: Yes, as long as you were the assigned teacher for that subject.

### Technical Questions

**Q: The app is slow. What should I do?**
A: Try closing other apps, connecting to better WiFi, or restarting your device.

**Q: I'm not receiving notifications. What's wrong?**
A: Check that notifications are enabled in your phone settings for the Dreamy app.

**Q: Can I use the app offline?**
A: No, an internet connection is required to use the app.
